## Decision models in stickler

Stickler's shapes are deterministic, and most lines they meet are
settled by the shapes alone. The honest seat for judgment is the
borderline verdict, a line the deterministic checker flags as
unsettled because the pattern sits between two readings and no
stated rule closes it. The contract will state which patterns count
as borderline, narrowly, so the border is itself a deterministic
test and never a feeling. Typesafe Jev is the primary route,
connected and verified live on 2026-11-02 through the programme's
own connector, answering a choice question with a chosen label, a
confidence, and a probability per label. The OpenAI decision model
is the comparison point, and the first borderline round records
both models over the same borderline fixtures before any adoption
widens beyond Jev.

Where judgment enters is the verdict on a flagged borderline line
and nowhere else. The call presents the line, its diff context in
stated summary form, and the shape readings the checker already
computed, and asks the model to choose between the labels stickler
and clean. A verdict below a confidence the contract will state is
an abstention, and an abstained line is reported as unjudged, never
silently clean, with the unjudged count standing in the review
beside the shape counts. The model's one line reason is recorded
beside the verdict, so a reader can see why the line was judged as
it was.

What the model decides is a choice per borderline line, and
nothing else. What stays deterministic is everything the checker
already does. The named shapes still draw their judgements and
their rewrites from the line alone. The validator still names
malformed reviews and never raises. The exit codes still fall out
of the recorded judgements by the stated arithmetic, and a recorded
verdict counts in that arithmetic exactly like a shape judgement,
so a finished review replays to the same exit code forever with no
live call.

Tests stay offline on recorded fixtures. A verdict call returns an
envelope holding the model name, the decision with its choice,
confidence, and probabilities, and the token usage, and that
envelope is what a fixture records. The suite replays recorded
verdicts through the same adapter the live slice uses, covering a
stickler verdict, a clean verdict, and an abstention, so the
borderline path is exercised with no live call in the gate. Live
calls happen only inside a gated live slice whose recordings then
join the fixtures.

The bounded live budget proposal is a cap of 200 verdict calls in
any one round. Borderline lines are a small fraction of any real
diff by construction, since the borderline patterns are stated
narrowly, and each call is small, carrying one line and its stated
context. The pilot round first measures the real per call token
cost over the recorded borderline fixtures before any live diff is
judged. Every live round logs its estimate before it runs and its
actual spend after, in the Spectacle line ledger, under the
standing cap of 50 dollars per repo per round that the owner set on
2026-11-02. The cap stated here is the
standing budget for this repo under the owner's ample budget
ruling of the same day, and a live round runs against it without
a further approval step. Raising the cap means amending this
section with the reason recorded, in the open, and the spend
ledger still closes every round.
